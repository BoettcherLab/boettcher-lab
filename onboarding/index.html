How to Use This Page

This page is your single reference for everything you need to do when joining the Boettcher Lab.

Print out the “Signature Sheet,” the last page of this document. Work through the phases in order. As you go, collect the necessary signatures. Finally, submit the completed form to Shannon for approval.

Only after this is complete, you may begin working independently in the lab.

Steps are labeled with the following tags where they don't apply to everyone:

  • [GRAD/POSTDOC] — not required for undergrads
  • [UNDERGRAD] — specific guidance for undergrads
  • [VISITING] — specific guidance for visiting students / researchers
  • [LBNL] — only for members working at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab
  • [UCB] — only for members working at the UC Berkeley campus (Tan Hall)
  • [OPTIONAL] — recommended but not required

What Could Go Wrong! (WCGW)

Share what went wrong and how to avoid it happening again. WCGW has two separate, anonymous learning logs: known equipment problems and recent safety lessons. Do not include people's names, identifying details, medical information, or confidential research.

Known Equipment Issues

Recurring instrument problems, error messages, malfunctioning equipment, workarounds, repair progress, and preventive maintenance. Each entry includes the equipment, location, issue description, troubleshooting, recommended prevention, and status (Ongoing or Resolved).

View Known Equipment Issues (Google Sheet)

For an active instrument problem, contact the designated superuser in the Equipment Everywhere + Superuser Spreadsheet.

Recent Safety Lessons Learned

Anonymous summaries of safety accidents and near misses, contributing conditions, immediate response, corrective actions, and lessons that can prevent future incidents. Each entry records status (Ongoing or Resolved) without identifying the people involved.

View Recent Safety Lessons Learned (Google Sheet)

Important: This educational log does not replace mandatory reporting to campus EH&S, LBNL, supervisors, or emergency services. Share only de-identified descriptions. Minor details may identify individuals in a small lab, even without names.

Anonymous Google Forms for these two sections are not yet connected. Once created, configure each form to avoid collecting email addresses or requiring sign-in; verify its privacy and sharing settings before accepting submissions. For now, coordinate log additions through a designated lab administrator.
