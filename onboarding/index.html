How to Use This Page

This page is your single reference for everything you need to do when joining the Boettcher Lab.

Print out the “Signature Sheet,” the last page of this document. Work through the phases in order. As you go, collect the necessary signatures. Finally, submit the completed form to Shannon for approval.

Only after this is complete, you may begin working independently in the lab.

Steps are labeled with the following tags where they don't apply to everyone:

  • [GRAD/POSTDOC] — not required for undergrads
  • [UNDERGRAD] — specific guidance for undergrads
  • [VISITING] — specific guidance for visiting students / researchers
  • [LBNL] — only for members working at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab
  • [UCB] — only for members working at the UC Berkeley campus (Tan Hall)
  • [OPTIONAL] — recommended but not required
